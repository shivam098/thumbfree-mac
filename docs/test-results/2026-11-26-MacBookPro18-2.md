into the built-in mic in two fresh TextEdit documents. The installed app received synthesized Right Option
taps; no audio was injected into the recognizers. Sample saving was off. Playback was set to 18% and
verified at most 20%; mic input volume was 50%. The output was re-muted before restoring its original level.

History entry and a sample from the same WAV. No personal History or sample preference was used.

before restoration. In each case the test target read the intended dictation once, all three privacy
markers were present, and the other process's copy survived. No system key was posted and the general
pasteboard, clipboard managers and Universal Clipboard were not exercised.
